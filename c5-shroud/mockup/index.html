The car body here is a simplified shape built for this mockup, not a scan, so its proportions are approximate. The carrier, shroud and pods are the real v2 design at true size, placed where they bolt on. Door angles are set by eye: about 13° at the sleepy stop and 48° full up. The outer pods turn out 8° to follow the curve of the headlight.
