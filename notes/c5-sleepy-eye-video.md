## What the look is (from the thumbnail)

- The pop-up headlight doors stay mostly closed. The door sits only slightly open,
  which gives the "sleepy eye".
- In the gap under the door's front edge sits a row of three small square LED
  projector cubes, side by side across the width of the opening.
- The cubes sit behind a dark surround, so only the three lit faces show.
- In the frame at about 75% of the video, the kit's units sit bagged on the bench:
  three cube-shaped lens housings joined into one assembly.

## How the kit works (from the chapter list)

1. **Pull the stock assembly:** raise the headlight by hand, then remove the bezel
   (Phillips screws), the headlight cover (four T15 screws), the 10mm adjuster nut and the
   10mm inner mounting bolt. Rotate the assembly down 90 degrees, then remove two 10mm
   bolts to lift it out.
2. **Note the stock bulbs:** mark which plug is high beam and which is low beam
   before unplugging.
3. **Two housing versions:** 1997–2000 (early) and 2000–2004 (later). The stop
   bracket installs differently on each, and the early version has an extra lower bolt
   to remove.
4. **Limit the door's travel:** detach the arm from the headlight motor, then fit
   the kit's metal stops so the motor can only open the door partway. Adjust the
   stop height until it contacts, then set the headlight height by hand. The stops
   create the sleepy-eye position; the LEDs don't.
5. **Wiring:**
   - Tap a 12V feed for the halo (ring light).
   - Connect the high- and low-beam plugs and check polarity.
   - Run a function test.
   - Optionally add a resistor. The chapter list suggests it deals with how the lights
     retract, and one chapter covers retracting without it.
   - Mount the resistor and secure the plugs.
6. **Reinstall:** mount the assembly and the inner mount, then install the rear
   damper.
7. **Optional relay:** a three-part relay install that lights all six LED
   cubes (three per side) on high beam.
8. The video ends with a troubleshooting section, starting at 1:19:00.

## What this means for a shroud design

- The shroud has to fit inside the partly open door. The stops set the opening, and
  step 18 sets it by hand, so the shroud's front opening should match that door
  height rather than the fully open position.
- The shroud frames three square lenses in a horizontal row. A dark finish matches
  the look in the thumbnail.
- The shroud must clear the high/low plugs and the halo wire tap. If the resistor
  and relay are used, it also has to clear the resistor mounted near the headlight.
- It has to work on both the 1997–2000 and 2000–2004 housings, or come in two versions.
- Measure the real opening on the car. The video's dimensions were not available here.
